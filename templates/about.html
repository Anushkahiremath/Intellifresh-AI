About FreshVision AI

FreshVision AI is a deep-learning-based image classification system designed to perform preliminary freshness assessment of selected fruits and vegetables.

The system uses a MobileNetV2 architecture fine-tuned for binary freshness classification and provides Fresh or Stale predictions together with model probabilities and explainable visual outputs where available.

Supported training distribution

Apple, Banana, Bitter Gourd, Capsicum, Orange and Tomato.

Predictions for categories outside the model's training distribution may be unreliable. The system is not a food-safety certification device.
