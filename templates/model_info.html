How results are produced

The same 224×224 RGB image is processed independently by two PyTorch models. EfficientNet-B0 identifies one of 76 produce labels. MobileNetV2 returns binary Fresh/Stale probabilities. Freshness coverage is marked Supported only for Apple, Banana, Bitter Gourd, Capsicum, Orange and Tomato; other detected categories are transparently marked Experimental.

No evaluation accuracy or category support is fabricated.

Dataset expansion and model training

The project includes a Freshness44 preparation pipeline targeting 45,000 unique labeled images. It removes exact image duplicates and groups recognizable capture sessions before splitting the data. Actual prepared counts are recorded in the dataset report.

The visual training pipeline fine-tunes MobileNetV2 using training-only augmentation, class-weighted loss, AdamW, learning-rate reduction and early stopping. Validation macro-F1 selects the checkpoint; the original model and candidate are compared on the same held-out test split. A completed evaluation is required before claiming improved accuracy. Candidate weights can be selected with FRESHNESS_MODEL_PATH after review.

The separate multimodal research model also requires measured temperature, humidity and shelf-life labels. Public visual images do not provide these measurements. See TRAINING_GUIDE.md in the project for the full explanation and commands.
